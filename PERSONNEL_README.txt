UKJSFG Personnel Dossiers

Upload these site files to your new branch (repository root, not the UKJSFG-main folder). Files changed: index.html. Files added: personnel.js, personnel.css. Existing photos from assets/ are reused. No changes to live GitHub Pages until merged.

To edit biographies, roles, qualifications or awards, change the relevant entry in UKJSFG_PERSONNEL inside personnel.js. Menzy awards are currently left unrecorded rather than invented; add verified awards later.

Click or keyboard-activate any Meet the Team card to open a dossier. Escape or X closes it.
